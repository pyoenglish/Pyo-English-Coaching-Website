GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Have a different question? Just send me a message.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

That's absolutely fine. Lessons can be adapted to your current level so you can build confidence step by step.

Are adult English lessons online?

Yes. Private adult English lessons are live online sessions costing S$60 for 60 minutes. Online group lessons cost S$30 per student for 90 minutes. Children's private lessons are in-person home visits only.

Where do children's lessons take place?

Children's private lessons are exclusively in-person home visits. Each lesson lasts 90 minutes and costs S$150. Contact me to discuss your child's needs and availability.

How do online group lessons work?

Group lessons take place online, last 90 minutes, and cost S$30 per student per lesson. Students practice speaking together through interactive activities and guided conversations. Contact us for schedules and available places.

Can lessons focus on a specific English skill?

Yes. Private adult coaching can focus on conversation, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, or other English goals. Children's lessons can focus on reading, writing, phonics, and school-related skills.
